Biodegradation of porous silicon, as a model you can interrogate

Degradation of a porous particle is governed by two coupled processes — dissolution of the solid skeleton and transport of dissolved species out of the pore network. This site lets you inspect literature datasets against that model, fit your own degradation curve, solve the complete coupled problem, and design a hypothetical particle with a target degradation time.

Nothing needs to be uploaded to browse. Start anywhere.
